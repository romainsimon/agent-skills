# Cold email writing

## When to invoke

Invoke when asked to write cold outreach emails (initial + follow-ups) for a product. Covers the per-contact drafting pass, not mass campaign infrastructure.

Typical triggers:
- "Draft cold emails to these 30 creators"
- "Write a first-touch email for [target]"
- "Rewrite this outreach email"
- "Plan a follow-up sequence"

## The golden rule

A cold email should read like **a peer invitation**, not a marketing pitch. The recipient should feel "a real person is asking for my honest take", not "a sales funnel is targeting me".

If it reads like something a SaaS product would blast from Mailchimp, it's wrong.

## Anatomy of the initial email

```
Subject: <thematic, 2-3 words, not a tagline>

Hi <First>,

<Hook: reason to reach out NOW. Product v2/release/new feature.>

<Product in one concrete sentence with examples in parens.>

<Specific personalization tied to THEIR company / work.>

Would love your feedback

<Sender first name>
```

### Subject lines

- **Do**: thematic, neutral, curious-making. "AI Filmmaking", "Creator affiliates", "Motion design + AI"
- **Don't**: product taglines, feature lists, urgency scripts. Not "Save 50% on AI video!", not "One place for all AI video models", not "Quick question".

### The opening

- **Do**: jump straight to the hook. "I'm building X and just released a v2 I thought you might like" is the pattern.
- **Don't**: drop their title back at them ("Senior Creative Editor at WPP Production"). Nobody likes their LinkedIn profile read out loud.
- **Don't**: fake warmth ("Hope life is treating you well"). It's transparent filler.
- **Sometimes**: one warm true line. "Saw a few of your colleagues on my list too" is OK because it's true and specific.

### The product line

- One concrete sentence. List 2-3 concrete examples in parentheses.
- Name the product once, let the link carry the rest.

Example, Melies pattern:
> You get all the best AI video and image models in one place (Seedance 2, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, Veo 3, etc.) + I plan to release Studio, which will allow "Vibe Filmmaking".

### The personalization line

- Reference their specific company / role / audience by name. `Any interest to try for <Company>?` is a safe shape.
- The personalization should be a **question**, not a statement. Statements feel rehearsed. Questions feel curious.

### The ask

Two things only:
1. A specific interest check tied to their context.
2. "Would love your feedback" (or equivalent).

Never end with "Sign up here" / "Book a demo" / "See pricing". Those are post-conversion CTAs, not cold-email CTAs.

### Sign-off

Just the first name. No "Best,", no "Thanks,", no "Cheers,", no title under it.

```
Would love your feedback

Judy
```

The ending "Would love your feedback" / "Any interest?" is the sign-off energy. Adding "Best," on top dilutes it.

## Follow-up sequence (only 2)

### FU1 (+4 days)

```
Hi <First>,

Did you get a chance to take a look?

<One new sentence: an angle, an update, or a specific observation relevant to their work.>

<Sender>
```

The FU1 line should not repeat the initial. It should add one new thought. Otherwise the recipient feels spammed.

### FU2 (+10 days)

```
Hi <First>,

Hey, just wanted to make sure you saw my last email.

Would you be interested in <specific ask tied to their context>?

<Sender>
```

FU2 is a soft checkin + one specific question. It is **not** a goodbye ("last ping, let me know if you want me to stop" = giving up energy; bad).

Three is the ceiling. Don't send a 4th, it's harassment territory.

## Rules to enforce

- **One sentence per paragraph.** Blank line between each. Makes the email skimmable on mobile.
- **No em dashes**, no hyphens as dashes. Only hyphens for compound words.
- **Never mix two CTAs in one thread.** Pick either "paid signup" or "affiliate" per contact. Mixed asks convert worse than either alone.
- **Never promise things that aren't true.** "Free credits on signup" is only OK if free credits actually exist. "Studio ships early May" only if it does.
- **Link the product name** to its landing page in the HTML version. Let the plain text carry the product name normally; auto-link it on the way out (or write `<a href=...>Product</a>` if composing HTML directly).

## Direct pitch vs affiliate program (rule of thumb)

- **Direct pitch** when the recipient is the **buyer/user**: agency, studio, corporate role, freelance consultant buying tools for their own work.
- **Affiliate pitch** when the recipient has an **audience**: content creator, YouTuber, newsletter writer, course instructor, filmmaker-influencer.
- **Gray zone rule**: default to direct. Bigger upside per conversion; affiliated folks can escalate to "got an affiliate link?" on their own.

## French variants

Use `tu`, not `vous`. `vous` feels corporate; the peer-invitation tone needs `tu`.

```
Salut <Prénom>,

Je développe <Produit> et je viens de sortir une v2 que je voulais te faire tester !

Tu as accès à <benefit en une phrase> (<exemples>) + <future feature si pertinent>.

Un intérêt à tester pour <Entreprise> ?

J'adorerais avoir ton retour

<Prénom>
```

## Worked examples

### English pros (direct pitch)

Subject: `AI Filmmaking`

```
Hi Chris,

I'm building Melies and recently released a v2 I thought you might like to try!

You get all the best AI video and image models in one place (Seedance 2, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, Veo 3, etc.) + I plan to release Studio, which will allow "Vibe Filmmaking".

Any interest to try for WPP Production?

Would love your feedback

Judy
```

### English creator (affiliate pitch)

Subject: `AI Filmmaking`

```
Hi Nathan,

I'm building Melies and recently released a v2 I thought you might like!

You get all the best AI video and image models in one place (Seedance 2, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, Veo 3, etc.) + I plan to release Studio, which will allow "Vibe Filmmaking".

We also just launched an affiliate program with recurring commission on referrals.

Any interest to try and potentially share with your audience?

Judy
```

### FU2 (any campaign)

Subject: `Re: AI Filmmaking`

```
Hi Chris,

Hey, just wanted to make sure you saw my last email.

Would you be interested in giving it a spin on one of your next AI cuts?

Judy
```

## Anti-patterns seen and fixed

| Bad | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Subject: `One place for all the AI video models` | Sounds like a product ad | `AI Filmmaking` (thematic) |
| `Senior Creative Editor at WPP Production, saw your title` | Reads like LinkedIn bot | Drop credential-drop, ask a direct question |
| `Hope life is treating you well.` | Boilerplate filler | Skip, go straight to hook |
| `Free credits on signup: melies.co` | Signup CTA + repeats product name | Drop, let the embedded product link carry it |
| `Last nudge. "Pass" and I stop.` | Giving-up energy, depressing close | FU2 = soft checkin + specific question |
| `Best, Romain` / `Cheers, Judy` | Adds salutation on top of closing line, dilutes it | Just `Judy` |
| `juggling ten subscriptions` + `juggling model accounts` | Repeating the same pain point twice | State once |
| Mixing "sign up for paid plan" + "join affiliate" in one email | Two CTAs = weaker than one | Pick one per contact |

## Quick checklist before sending

- [ ] Subject is thematic, under 30 chars?
- [ ] First sentence is a hook, not "hope you're well"?
- [ ] Product described in one sentence with concrete examples?
- [ ] One personalization line tied to their company/role?
- [ ] Ask is a question, not a CTA?
- [ ] Sign-off is just the first name, no "Best,"?
- [ ] One sentence per paragraph, blank lines between?
- [ ] No em dashes or hyphens-as-dashes?
- [ ] No "free/sign up/book a demo/pricing" language?
- [ ] Product name linked in the HTML version?
- [ ] Under ~80 words for the initial?
